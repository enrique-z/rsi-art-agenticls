# Domain-semantics audit — agenticls-rsi-leakage

**Purpose.** This document records the author self-audit of every chemistry and
pharmacology reasoning claim in the manuscript. The anti-fabrication guard verifies
that every numeric literal in `main.tex` matches a ledger row, but nothing in that
guard verifies that the *chemistry and pharmacology reasoning around* those numbers is
correct: whether logD and logS are on the sensible scale, whether a Tanimoto
near-duplicate implies a transferable label, whether an AUC can move in the direction
the paper claims on 25 molecules, whether "BBB penetration" is even extractable from
ChEMBL activity rows. That is what this file records.

three rival mechanisms and one discriminator falsifier. All are addressed in the
manuscript's own **§6 Rival mechanisms** section and are recorded in `CLAIMS.md`.

  in §5 R1 (control arm B gap +0.2496 against the ChEMBL-trained +0.4093) and in
  §Limitations ¶2.

  1.6181 for random pairs, paired difference −1.5372 [−1.7484, −1.3339]); the

  reported in the abstract, §5 R2, and CLAIMS.md.
